A must-read for every software developer. This book covers practical advice and practices that every programmer should know.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Write code that works, that makes sense, and that other people can understand
- Learn to make trade-offs in your design
- Continuously learn and improve your craft

**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

This book completely changed how I approach software development. The concepts of DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself) and being pragmatic are now fundamental to my work.
